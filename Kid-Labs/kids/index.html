Methodological & Boundary Disclosure (GHG Protocol / ISO 14064-1 & ISO 14067 Principles):
  • Boundary & Non-Certification: This calculator provides an educational, indicative child-level estimate and is not an ISO 14064-1 certified inventory. Student travel to school is categorised as Student School Commuting (not corporate Scope 3 Category 7 Employee Commuting).
  • Shared Household & Device Allocation: Household electricity (CEA India Grid Baseline v20.0, 2024: 0.727 kg CO₂e/kWh) is allocated using an equal per-capita household share (1 ÷ household size) so whole-household consumption is never attributed to a child. Shared digital devices are allocated across users, and active device charging is not double-counted when household electricity is provided.
  • Geographical & Proxy Limitations: India-specific emission factors (CEA, India GHG Program, CGIAR/FAO) are used for grid power, road/rail transport, and diet; international lifecycle proxies (ISO 14067 literature, DEFRA/DESNZ, EXIOBASE) are used for school supplies and embodied electronics.
